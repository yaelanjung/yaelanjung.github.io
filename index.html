About

Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher working with Daniel Dilks at Emory University.

How do we recognize the places around us and find our way through the world? My research investigates the neural systems that support human spatial cognition, from recognizing what kind of place we are in and navigating our immediate surroundings to building knowledge of the broader spatial world.

I am particularly interested in how the brain represents different kinds of spatial information, how these representations support recognition and navigation, and how the underlying neural systems emerge and develop. To address these questions, I combine human neuroimaging, behavioral experiments, and computational approaches, studying spatial cognition from infancy through adulthood.
